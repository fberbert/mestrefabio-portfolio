## Design Context

### Users
Two audiences with equal weight:
- Recruiters and tech leads evaluating Fábio for a remote senior role (full stack, applied AI, Linux/AWS). They skim fast, want proof of seniority (shipped products, real companies, stack depth) and the resume PDF.
- Prospective clients looking to hire a project (freelance/contract). They want to see what he has built, judge whether he can solve their problem and reach him easily.

Job to be done: within a minute, understand who Fábio is, see credible shipped work, and either download the resume or make contact.

### Brand Personality
Experienced and reliable, technical and direct, creative with a sense of humor ("Mestre Fábio", the talking plush toy, an AI-made music video, a fighting game made from friends' photos). 26 years of practice, founder of Viva o Linux since 2002. Bilingual site (PT-BR and EN).

Emotional goals: confidence in his seniority first, then curiosity and a smile from the playful projects.

### Aesthetic Direction
Bold and memorable; a large change from the current template look is acceptable. Must keep both light and dark themes. Current state: Next.js + Tailwind, Inter font, purple/blue blurred blobs, looping code video background, centered single column, rounded cards (inspired by ByteGrad's portfolio template). No explicit references or anti-references given.

### Design Principles
1. Proof over claims: shipped products, real companies, numbers and screenshots carry the page.
2. Two fast exits: resume download and contact are always easy to reach.
3. Seniority first, personality second: humor lives in projects and details, never at the cost of credibility.
4. Distinct, not templated: the site should not read as a generic developer portfolio template.
5. Bilingual and theme parity: every change works in PT and EN, light and dark.
